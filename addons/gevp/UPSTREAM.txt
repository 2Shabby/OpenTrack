Excluded: demo scenes, controllers, cameras, effects, and sounds.

Local changes in scripts/wheel.gd:
- Initialize previous_global_position at scene entry, preventing spawn-position
  dependent velocity spikes on the first physics tick.
- Initialize visual spring length at full extension.
- Pass body-relative world offsets to apply_force for the wheel torque couple,
  as required by Godot's RigidBody3D API.
- Apply rolling drag separately from tire reaction torque so coasting tires do
  not cancel surface resistance. Limit drag to the contact's stopping impulse
  to avoid reversing motion near zero speed.

Local changes in scripts/vehicle.gd:
- Support an optional front_center_wheel instead of the front pair. Shared
  axle processing handles one front wheel or the original pair, including
  weight, spring/damping rates, brakes, drivetrain, steering and center of mass.
  The centered wheel has no opposite wheel, antiroll or Ackermann correction.
  Existing four-wheel scenes retain their original layout and tuning.

Formatting: removed trailing whitespace in both scripts; no behavior change.
